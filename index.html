01 / THE OPPORTUNITY

When visibility fails,
logistics feels it.

Vayu is built around a simple operational problem: a fleet manager needs to know not only where a vehicle is, but whether the vehicle can reliably perceive its surroundings.

THE PROBLEM

Weather changes faster than operational decisions.

Fog, heavy rain, glare and low-light conditions can reduce the reliability of camera-based perception. A warning alone is not enough when the fleet also needs a response.

Visibility drops→Perception uncertainty→Operational risk

APPENDIX / THINKING & AI USE

How the idea
changed.

This section documents the decision that shaped the product: how autonomous should a safety-critical AI system be?

OPTION A

Fully autonomous

Maximum automation, but the safety and liability exposure grows sharply when perception itself becomes uncertain.

REJECTED
OPTION B

Advisory only

Safer and simpler, but much of the operational value of an agentic workflow is lost.

MODIFIED
OPTION C

Controlled autonomy

Automate low-risk digital workflows; escalate safety-critical uncertainty to humans or validated vehicle systems.
