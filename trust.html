APPENDIX / DEVELOPMENT RECORD

What I accepted, changed & rejected.

The hardest design question was how much autonomy to allow in a safety-critical perception system.

REJECTED

Fully autonomous

Too much safety and liability exposure when perception itself is uncertain.

MODIFIED

Advisory only

Safer, but loses much of the value of an agentic workflow.

ACCEPTED

Controlled autonomy

Automate low-risk workflows and escalate safety-critical uncertainty.
